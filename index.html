ARRVD Family arrival app for iPhone

Know they arrived.
Not everywhere they went.

ARRVD tells you when someone in your family arrives at the places that matter — home, school, lessons — and lets you know if an arrival hasn’t been confirmed by the expected time. No live map. No movement history.

How it works

Reassurance, kept simple.

  • Know they arrived

    See when a family member arrives at a place your family has registered — home, school, lessons or work.

  • Get the notices that matter

    Organizers are notified of arrivals, and of arrivals not yet confirmed by the expected time. Departure notices can be turned on for each place.

  • Privacy, without constant tracking

    No live map and no movement history. Your family sees arrivals at and departures from registered places — and those records are deleted after 7 days.

How it works

  1. 1

    Register places

    An Organizer adds the places that matter, such as home or school, and chooses which family members each place is for.

  2. 2

    Set the expected arrival

    Set the days and time someone is expected to arrive at the place.

  3. 3

    See arrivals — or a notice

    When the Member’s iPhone detects the arrival, the Organizer is notified. If it isn’t confirmed by the expected time, they get a “not yet confirmed” notice instead.

Family members join with a one-time invitation link and choose for themselves whether to share.

Reassurance, privacy and consent

How location is used

ARRVD does use location. On the Member’s iPhone, iOS Location Services detect arrivals at and departures from registered places — even when the app isn’t open, if location access is set to Always. ARRVD doesn’t show anyone’s live location or create a movement history.

Joining with consent

Organizers are 18 or over and Members 13 or over; people under 13 can’t use ARRVD. A Member aged 13 to 17 shares nothing until a parent or guardian and the Member have both agreed. Members can stop sharing at any time.

Who it’s for

ARRVD is an iPhone app for people who live in Malaysia and Japan. It has a free plan.

Honest about limits

Detection depends on iOS, the iPhone’s settings and its signal, so an arrival may be detected late or not at all. ARRVD isn’t an emergency service.
