2. What ARRVD does

ARRVD helps a family confirm that a family member has arrived at a place the family has set up — for example, school, an activity or work. You choose the places and, if you like, the expected arrival times; ARRVD lets the family know when an arrival is confirmed, or when it hasn’t been confirmed by the expected time. ARRVD is not designed to show anyone’s live location.

3. Your account

You sign in to ARRVD with Sign in with Apple. Keep your Apple Account and device secure, and use ARRVD only with your own account. You are responsible for activity that happens through your account.

4. Families, Organizers and Members

  • An Organizer creates a family, invites people to join, adds and manages places, chooses which Members each place checks arrival for, and receives arrival notifications.
  • A Member joins a family through an invitation. A Member’s device confirms their arrivals at the places they are assigned to and shares those arrivals with the family.
  • If you invite or add someone, you must have the appropriate authority or consent to do so — for example, as a parent or guardian — and let them know how ARRVD works. You must not add anyone to a family, or monitor their arrivals, without that authority or consent.
  • Information shared within a family is visible to that family as described in the Privacy Policy.

5. Limits of arrival detection

Arrival detection and notifications depend on things outside ARRVD’s control, including:

  • GPS and location accuracy, and how iOS handles location and region monitoring;
  • whether the app can run in the background, and the device’s battery, power-saving and other settings;
  • location and notification permissions;
  • network connections; and
  • services provided by Apple, Google Firebase and others.

As a result, an arrival may be detected late or not at all, a detection may be wrong, a “not yet confirmed” notice may be sent even though the person did arrive, and a notification may be delayed or not delivered. ARRVD does not guarantee that every arrival will be detected or reported.
