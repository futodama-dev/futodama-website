ARRVD

For guardians

A child aged 13 to 17 can share their arrivals and departures in ARRVD only after a parent or guardian has agreed, and the child has agreed too. This page shows what you agree to.

Explanation version 2027-01-v2 · the same text the app shows · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Where this page says “the child”, the app shows your child’s display name.

1. Who can consent

  • You must be 18 or over and the child’s parent, legal guardian or a person with parental responsibility.
  • Being an organizer of the family is not the same as being the guardian. An organizer who isn’t the guardian can’t consent for the child.
  • Your consent is a declaration you make with your own Apple account. It is not an official identity check; organizers are told when it takes effect and can object if they don’t recognise you.

2. How to give consent

  1. Your child sends you a guardian link from the ARRVD app (one use, valid for 7 days).
  2. Install ARRVD on your iPhone, open it and tap “For guardians”. Sign in with your own Apple account (not your child’s).
  3. Paste the whole link you received. Opening the link in a web browser doesn’t open the request.
  4. Read the explanation (Japanese, English or Malay), choose your relationship, enter your name and tick the three declarations.
  5. Choose which of the family’s organizers can see your child’s records. They see them only from when you allow it.

If you are already an organizer in the same family, you can also review the request in the app under Settings › Guardian requests and consents.

3. The explanation you agree to

What ARRVD is

ARRVD tells a family's organizers when a member arrives at or leaves places the family has registered (such as home or school).

What is shared, and who can see it

The child's arrivals at and departures from the registered places, and arrivals not confirmed by the expected time. Only the organizers you allow after consenting can see them, from when you allow them; organizers you haven't allowed can't. You choose each organizer and can stop it at any time. If you are also an organizer of this family, you can see them too, from your consent on; your own access can't be stopped on its own — to stop it, withdraw your consent.

What is not shared

No live location and no route sharing.

What is stored, and for how long

We store the registered places, the records of arrivals, departures and arrivals not confirmed by the expected time (deleted automatically after 7 days), and the record of this consent (who it covers, the version and language of this explanation, the date, and the relationship and name you give). The consent record is kept while the consent is valid and for 3 years after it ends (for example, when it is withdrawn). If it is needed for a complaint or legal proceedings, we keep it until that is resolved. Your name is erased when your account is deleted.

Handling outside your country

Information is handled on Google's cloud (database and server processing in Singapore, sign-in processing in the United States, notification delivery on facilities in various countries) and through Apple's notification and sign-in services, to provide watching, send notifications and prevent misuse. Who receives the child's information outside your country (Google and Apple), the privacy laws of those countries and the measures the recipients take are explained under “International processing” in the Privacy Policy (link below). Your consent also covers this provision of the child's information (in Japan, depending on age and understanding, a legal representative's consent is needed — generally for children aged 12 to 15 or younger).

Privacy Policy: International processing

Withdrawing

You can withdraw at any time. In the app (Settings > Guardian requests and consents), sharing stops right away. You can also withdraw in writing by email to futodama0610@gmail.com. An email does not stop sharing by itself: we read it, stop sharing promptly by hand and reply to confirm. Records arriving after sharing stops are refused by our servers.

Their own consent

The child also has to agree to sharing themselves.

4. What you declare

  • I am 18 or over
  • I am this person's parent, guardian or a person with parental responsibility
  • I understand the above and agree

Relationship (choose one)

  • Parent
  • Legal guardian
  • Person with parental responsibility

5. Who can see your child’s records

Agreeing doesn’t let you see the family, its places or its activity (if you are also an organizer of the same family, you can see your child’s records from your consent on). You decide which organizers can see your child’s arrivals, departures and arrivals not confirmed by the expected time, and can stop any of them at any time in Settings › Guardian requests and consents › Who can see. Notifications already on an organizer’s phone can’t be taken back.

6. Withdrawing consent

  • In the app: Settings › Guardian requests and consents › Withdraw consent.
  • By email, if you can’t use the app: write to futodama0610@gmail.com with the family name, the child’s display name and your relationship. An email does not stop sharing by itself: we read it, stop sharing promptly by hand and reply to confirm (if we can’t tell which child it is, we reply to ask).

What happens when you withdraw: Sharing of this person's arrivals and departures stops right away, and organizers can't see new records. Existing records are deleted when their usual retention period (7 days) ends. A record of this withdrawal is kept for 3 years from the withdrawal (longer only while it is needed for a complaint or legal proceedings). If you can't use the app, you can also withdraw by email to futodama0610@gmail.com (an email does not stop sharing by itself: we read it and stop sharing promptly by hand).

7. When your child turns 18

When your child asks in the app, you can confirm that they have turned 18. Your consent then ends and only their own consent decides what is shared. No date of birth or ID is asked for this confirmation, and it doesn’t change past records (they are still deleted after 7 days).

8. Questions

For questions, or to ask to see, correct or delete your child’s information, email futodama0610@gmail.com. See also the ARRVD Privacy Policy.
