Product

ARRVD

Know they arrived.
Not everywhere they went.

A privacy-conscious family arrival app designed to confirm important arrivals without continuous location tracking.

Coming soon

Arrival reassurance without constant tracking.

Families often want to know one simple thing: "Did they arrive?" ARRVD is designed around that moment. Instead of centering the experience on continuous location tracking, it focuses on confirming expected arrivals.

How it works

1

Set a place

Choose a place you'd like to confirm arrival at — school, an activity, work, and more.

2

Set the expected time

Set the day and time someone is expected to arrive.

3

Get reassurance

Get confirmation on arrival — and a notice if they haven't arrived by the expected time.

Why ARRVD

Arrival, not surveillance

Focused on confirming arrival, not continuous location tracking.

Privacy-conscious by design

Designed with privacy in mind from the beginning.

Simple when it matters

No extra features — just the information that matters, simply delivered.

Privacy by design

ARRVD is being designed to minimize unnecessary location exposure and to focus the user experience on arrival confirmation rather than continuous movement tracking.

Full privacy and data details will be published alongside the app before launch.

Frequently asked questions

Does ARRVD continuously show live location?

No. ARRVD is designed around confirming arrivals rather than displaying continuous, real-time movement.

Is ARRVD available yet?

Not yet. ARRVD is currently in development and is not yet available to download.

Who is ARRVD designed for?

Families who want reassurance that someone arrived somewhere expected, such as school, an activity, or work.

Will ARRVD support Android?

Platform availability beyond the initial launch is planned but not yet announced.

How is privacy considered?

ARRVD is being designed to minimize unnecessary location exposure. Full privacy details will be published before launch.
