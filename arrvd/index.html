ARRVD

Know they arrived.
Not everywhere they went.

Know when someone arrives where they are expected — without following everywhere they go.

Coming soon

Reassurance without constant tracking.

ARRVD is designed around a simple idea: families should be able to know that someone arrived safely without needing to continuously follow their location.

How it works

1

Set a place

Choose a place you'd like to confirm arrival at — school, an activity, work, and more.

2

Set the expected time

Set the day and time someone is expected to arrive.

3

Get reassurance

Get confirmation on arrival — and a notice if they haven't arrived by the expected time.

Privacy by design

ARRVD isn't built around a live map or continuous location tracking. It's designed to answer one simple question — did they arrive? — rather than showing everywhere someone goes.

Full privacy and data details will be published alongside the app before launch.

Why ARRVD

Arrival, not surveillance

Focused on confirming arrival, not continuous location tracking.

Reassurance with privacy

Reassurance and privacy, together.

Simple by design

No extra features — just the information that matters, simply delivered.
