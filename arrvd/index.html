Family arrival app for iPhone

ARRVD

Know they arrived.
Not everywhere they went.

ARRVD tells you when someone in your family arrives at the places that matter, and lets you know if an arrival hasn’t been confirmed by the expected time — without a live map or a movement history.

Free · iPhone (iOS 15 or later) · Japan and Malaysia

How it works

Arrival reassurance without constant tracking.

Families often want to know one simple thing: “Did they arrive?” ARRVD is designed around that moment. Instead of showing where someone is all the time, it confirms arrivals at the places your family registers — and tells you when an expected arrival hasn’t been confirmed.

How it works

1

Register places

An Organizer adds the places that matter, such as home or school, and chooses which family members each place is for.

2

Set the expected arrival

Set the days and time someone is expected to arrive at the place.

3

See arrivals — or a notice

When the Member’s iPhone detects the arrival, the Organizer is notified. If it isn’t confirmed by the expected time, they get a “not yet confirmed” notice instead.

Family members join with a one-time invitation link and choose for themselves whether to share. Departure notices can be turned on for each place.

Why ARRVD

Arrival, not surveillance

Focused on arrivals at registered places — no live map and no movement history.

Privacy-conscious by design

Records of arrivals, departures and arrivals not yet confirmed are deleted automatically after 7 days.

Simple when it matters

No extra features — just the arrivals and notices that matter, simply delivered.

Privacy by design

ARRVD does use location. On the Member’s iPhone, iOS Location Services (mainly region monitoring) detect arrivals at and departures from the places assigned to that Member — even when the app isn’t open, if location access is set to Always. The iPhone sends a record of the arrival or departure, not its coordinates or a route, and ARRVD doesn’t show anyone’s live location.

A Member shares only after choosing to in the app, and can stop at any time. Organizers are 18 or over and Members 13 or over; a Member aged 13 to 17 shares nothing until a parent or guardian and the Member have both agreed (see For guardians).

What ARRVD processes, and what it doesn’t, is set out in the ARRVD Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Does ARRVD continuously show live location?

No. ARRVD is designed around confirming arrivals rather than displaying continuous, real-time movement. It doesn’t create a movement history.

Does ARRVD use location at all?

Yes. The Member’s iPhone uses iOS Location Services to detect arrivals at and departures from registered places, including in the background when location access is set to Always. Only the arrival or departure record is shared with the family.

Is ARRVD available yet?

Yes. ARRVD is available on the App Store for iPhone, free, in Japan and Malaysia. Open ARRVD on the App Store.

Who is ARRVD designed for?

Families who want reassurance that someone arrived somewhere expected, such as school, an activity, or work. ARRVD serves people who live in Malaysia and Japan. Organizers are 18 or over and Members 13 or over.

Is ARRVD free?

ARRVD has a free plan. ARRVD Plus, a paid plan, can be purchased in the app once it is offered; the current limits of each plan are shown in the app.

Will ARRVD detect every arrival?

Not always. Detection depends on iOS, the iPhone’s settings and its signal, so an arrival may be detected late or not at all, and a “not yet confirmed” notice may come even though the person arrived. ARRVD isn’t an emergency service. See Support for the settings that help.

Will ARRVD support Android?

ARRVD is an iPhone app. Platform availability beyond the initial launch is planned but not yet announced.

How is privacy considered?

ARRVD is designed to minimize unnecessary location exposure. It uses location on the device to detect arrivals at the places your family sets up, without showing anyone’s live location. See the ARRVD Privacy Policy for details.

Get ARRVD

ARRVD is available on the App Store for iPhone. It’s free to start.
