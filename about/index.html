About futodama

futodama is an independent product studio creating simple digital experiences that make everyday life a little better.

Why we exist

We exist to make everyday inconveniences, worries, joys, and small discoveries a little better through simple products.

What we make

We build clear, lightweight digital experiences that stay close to how people actually feel — from reassurance and convenience to joy and reflection.

How we build

We build calmly and carefully, favoring clarity over cleverness and warmth over hype. Nothing is exaggerated, and nothing is designed to create unnecessary worry.

What we value

Reassurance

A little confidence that things are okay, without needing constant checking.

Joy

Small, genuine moments of delight in everyday use.

Empathy

Designing with real feelings and real situations in mind.

Simplicity

Clear over clever, every time.

Lightness

Technology that feels light to carry, not heavy.

Gratitude

An appreciation for the small things that make days better.
