Product

Mojitsunagi

Connect letters, discover words, and grow your forest.

What it is

A Japanese word puzzle combining falling blocks and connected-word discovery. Letters fall into place, and it's up to you to connect them into words.

Gameplay idea

Connect letters as they fall to discover Japanese words, clearing blocks and building combos along the way.

Forest growth concept

Every word you discover helps a small forest grow — a quiet, ongoing reward for staying curious.
